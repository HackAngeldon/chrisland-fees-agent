# Going live: a note for the school

**What it does.** Parents message the school's WhatsApp number. The assistant checks who they are (student ID and the phone on file), says what is owed and by when, and sends a Paystack link for the balance or a part payment your rules allow. When they reply "paid", it checks with Paystack and sends a receipt. Each morning the accounts office gets a list of who owes, overdue families first, with reminders drafted for you to send.

**What it does not do.** Record bank drafts, give discounts or extensions, issue refunds or settle disputes: it hands those to your accounts office. It answers only about fees.

**Before real parents use it:**
1. Your own verified Paystack account and live keys. The demo borrows a test account, so its checkout says "Ordina".
2. Your WhatsApp number connected to Chord, with Meta-approved templates for reminders.
3. Real student, class and parent-phone records, and parents told how their number is used (Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023).
4. Your fee rules confirmed: part payments, minimums, deadlines, and whether parents or the school pay Paystack's fee.
5. A paid server plan, and a named accounts officer who answers handed-over chats in working hours.

**When something goes wrong.** A failed payment is never recorded: the parent is told no money moved and is offered a new link. If Paystack or the server is down, or the payment key stops working, the assistant stops offering links and hands the chat to the office.

**Who is alerted.** The accounts officer sees every handed-over chat in Chord. Set an uptime check that emails your IT contact when the server stops answering, and send Paystack's dispute emails to the bursar.

**Start carefully.** Run the first week on "Suggest, I approve", so the office sees every reply before it goes out.
