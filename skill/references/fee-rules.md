# Fee rules, 2026/2027 session (demo)

The tools apply these rules; this page is so you can explain them. When this page and a tool disagree, the tool is right.

## Terms

|---|---|---|---|
| First term | 7 September 2026 | 30 October 2026 | 11 December 2026 |
| Second term | 11 January 2027 | 12 February 2027 | 1 April 2027 |
| Third term | 26 April 2027 | 28 May 2027 | 16 July 2027 |

## What a term costs

- **Tuition, every term:** nursery ₦750,000, primary ₦900,000, secondary ₦1,100,000.
- **First term only:** development levy (nursery ₦150,000, primary ₦250,000, secondary ₦300,000) and books and materials (nursery ₦100,000, primary ₦150,000, secondary ₦200,000).
- **New students, first term only:** registration ₦50,000 (one-time, non-refundable) and a caution fee of ₦100,000 (refundable when the student leaves, if school rules were kept and no property was damaged).
- **Chairman Scholarship:** covers tuition in full. The levy, books and other items are still payable.

So a returning primary pupil pays ₦1,300,000 in first term and ₦900,000 in each later term: ₦3,100,000 for the session.

## Paying

- Fees are due **on or before the first day of each term**.
- Parents may pay **termly or for the whole session**. Paying the whole session's fees in full by the first day of first term earns a **5% discount on tuition**. For 2026/2027 that window closed on 7 September 2026; it opens again for 2027/2028.
- **Part payments (instalments):** up to **two payments per term**. A part payment must be at least **₦100,000**, unless it settles the balance. The second payment must clear the term, and a part-paid term must be cleared by the date in the table.
- **Paying ahead:** once this term is fully paid, the parent can pay next term early.
- **Online payment** is by Paystack link: card, bank transfer or USSD. The school still accepts **certified bank drafts** made payable to Chrisland Schools, Lagos, at the accounts office; those are recorded by the office, not by you.

## What "status" means in a tool answer

- `paid`: nothing owed for the term.
- `not_due`: the term has not started; fees are due on its first day.
- `part_paid`: some paid, the rest due by the clear-by date.
- `overdue`: unpaid after the first day of term, or part-paid after the clear-by date. `days_overdue` counts from that date.

## Demo assumptions to confirm with the school

Chrisland publishes its due dates, termly or annual payment, the annual discount, and that registration is non-refundable and caution refundable. It does not publish the discount's size, an instalment policy, or late-payment penalties. The 5%, the two-payment rule, the ₦100,000 minimum and the clear-by dates are assumptions for this demo, and the agent applies **no late-payment penalty**.
