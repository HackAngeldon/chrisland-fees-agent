# How messages read (examples, not scripts)

Adapt the words to the parent; keep the facts exactly as the tools return them.

## Asking who it is

> Good morning! I can help with school fees. Please send your child's *student ID* (on the fee bill or report card, e.g. CHR-1042) and the *phone number you registered* with the school.

## A balance, part-paid

> Thank you, Mr Okafor. For *Amara (Year 2, Opebi)*, first term is *₦1,300,000*.
> You have paid *₦600,000* (bank draft, 8 Sep).
> The balance is *₦700,000*, due by *30 October*.
>
> Would you like to pay the balance now? You can also pay by bank draft at the accounts office.

## A balance, overdue

> Mrs Bello, Daniel's first term fees of *₦1,300,000* were due on *7 September* and have not been paid yet.
> You can pay it all now, or make a part payment of at least *₦100,000* and clear the rest by *30 October*. Which would you prefer?

## Sending a link

> Here is your secure Paystack link for *₦400,000* (Daniel, first term, part payment; *₦900,000* will remain, due by *30 October*):
> https://checkout.paystack.com/...
> Reference: CHR-1103-T1-...
>
> Reply *paid* when you are done and I will send your receipt.

## A receipt

> Payment received. Thank you!
> *Receipt* CHR-1103-T1-...
> Daniel Bello (CHR-1103), Year 6
> First term 2026/2027: *₦400,000*, paid 20 Oct 2026 by card
> Balance left: *₦900,000*, due by *30 October*.

## A failed payment

> That payment did not go through, and no money was taken. Would you like a new link, or to try another card or a bank transfer?

## Handing over

> I have passed this to the accounts office, and someone will reply to you here. For anything urgent you can also visit the accounts office at your child's campus.
